## App Store inceleme notu (App Review Information → Notes)
Yokuş Yolu is a single-player, offline hill climbing game. No account or login is needed; there are no ads, no in-app purchases and no data collection. On first launch the game asks for the language (Turkish/English). Tap "Yola çık / Hit the road" and hold the right pedal (GAS); the left pedal is BRAKE. All stages and vehicles can also be unlocked with in-game coins (akçe) earned by playing; no real money is involved inside the app. Settings (gear icon) contains How to Play, Rate, Feedback (opens mail), Recommend to a friend and Delete my data.
