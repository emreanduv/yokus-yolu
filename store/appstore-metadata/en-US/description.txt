A physics-based hill climbing race: hit the gas, conquer the slopes, keep your balance in the air and go as far as you can without flipping over!

Keloğlan is at the wheel. Drive five vehicles, from a noble sixties sedan and a boxy eighties legend to a farm tractor and a centenary electric SUV, across the most beautiful roads of Türkiye and the Turkic world.

STAGES
• Cappadocia: fairy chimneys and sunrise balloons
• Pamukkale: white travertine terraces and ancient columns
• Black Sea Highlands: mist, pine forests and tea gardens
• Foot of Mount Ararat: a snowy peak and slippery climbs
• Kazakh Steppe: golden grassland, yurts and horses
• Lake Issyk-Kul: the Tian Shan peaks and a turquoise lake

VEHICLES
• Devran 61: balanced and sturdy
• Şahan 131: strong and steady
• Anadal A1: light fibreglass body, nimble and quick
• Toprak 55: slow, but climbs anything
• Tagg T10: electric, silent, instant torque (collects charge instead of fuel)

GAMEPLAY
• Two pedals: GAS and BRAKE. In the air, gas tilts you back and brake tilts you forward.
• Grab fuel cans and akçe coins; earn bonuses for flips and long jumps.
• Upgrade engine, suspension, tires and tank in the garage.
• Every stage’s goal is 1923 metres: reach it to unlock the next stage.
• Turkish and English, no ads, works offline, collects no data.
