A physics-based hill climbing race: 1923 levels, 10 stages, 5 legendary rides. Hit the gas, conquer the slopes, keep your balance in the air and complete each level’s goal without flipping over!

Keloğlan is at the wheel. The road runs from the fairy chimneys of Cappadocia to Pamukkale, the Black Sea highlands, Mount Ararat, Nemrut and Lake Van, then on to the Kazakh steppe, Lake Issyk-Kul, Samarkand and the Altai Mountains.

1923 LEVELS
• Every level has a goal: distance, akçe coins, flips, airtime or a road without fuel cans.
• Finish fast for three stars. Each stage ends with the 1923-metre milestone.
• The first 10 levels are free. The Full Version (one-time purchase) opens the whole road.

STAGES
Cappadocia, Pamukkale, Black Sea Highlands, Foot of Mount Ararat, Mount Nemrut, Lake Van, Kazakh Steppe, Lake Issyk-Kul, Samarkand, Altai Mountains.

VEHICLES
• Devran 61: balanced and sturdy
• Şahan 131: strong and steady
• Taros 12: Anatolia’s white legend, tough underbody
• Anadal A1: light fibreglass body, nimble and quick
• Toprak 55: slow, but climbs anything
• Tagg T10: electric, silent, instant torque

ROADSIDE FRIENDS
A Malaklı dog waiting by the road, pearl mullet leaping in Lake Van and an odd-eyed Van cat at the finish line.

GAMEPLAY
• Two pedals: GAS and BRAKE. In the air, gas tilts you back and brake tilts you forward.
• Upgrade engine, suspension, tires and tank in the garage.
• Free ride: drive without a goal and beat your record.
• Turkish and English, no ads, works offline, collects no data.
